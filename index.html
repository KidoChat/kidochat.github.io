KidoChat is a chat app for children. Kids talk with friends their parents have approved, and parents can see every message. It is built by a parent and child, and is currently being tested by family and friends on iPhone and Android.

How it works

Parents approve every friend

A chat opens only after both families say yes. Adults who want to talk with a child need that child's parent to agree.

Unkind messages are stopped

Messages between kids are checked automatically before they arrive. Anything insulting, aggressive, rude or leaving someone out is held back for a parent to look at.

No photos between kids

Kids send each other text and emoji. Pictures, videos and voice notes go only to their own parents.

Quiet hours and SOS

Parents set quiet hours for each child. One SOS button sends a child's parents an alert with their location.

Get the app

Android

  1. On the phone, open the KidoChat test link and tap Become a tester.
  2. Then install KidoChat from Google Play.
  3. Open KidoChat and tap I'm a grown-up to create a grown-up account.

A test version, through Google Play's testing.

iPhone

  1. On the iPhone, install KidoChat from the App Store.
  2. Open KidoChat and tap I'm a grown-up to create a grown-up account.

In the App Store in Israel.

Kids don't sign up themselves: a grown-up adds each child inside their own account, then sets up the child's phone or tablet with a short code from the app. Kids have no password.

Privacy

KidoChat has no ads and does not sell or share personal information for advertising. Messages are stored in the European Union. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is collected and why.
