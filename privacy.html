Privacy policy

KidoChat · Effective 31 October 2026 · Updated 21 November 2026

KidoChat is a messaging app for children and the grown-ups around them. It is run by Atany LLC (30 N Gould St, STE 35224, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA), which is responsible for the information below. Contact: kidochat@atanyco.com (this also reaches our contact in Israel).

Children

Only a parent can create a child's account, from their own account, confirming they are the parent or legal guardian and agreeing to this policy and the terms for the child (we record when, and which version). The parent enters the child's full name, a nickname (the name others see) and a secret code, and sets up the child's phone. Parents approve every contact and profile picture, can read every message their child sends or receives, and can delete the child's account at any time. No ads. Children can't share photos, videos or voice notes with other children; their drawings are checked first.

Full names

Everyone is shown by nickname. A full name is seen only by the person, their family, people connected to them (friends and their parents, people in their groups, connected grown-ups) while the connection lasts, a parent or group admin deciding a request (for a suggested friend, only once both families agree), and KidoChat staff. Friend suggestions show a nickname and picture, never a full name. Nobody can search by full name. A request to connect sent from a group shows the asker's nickname, never their email or phone. Full names are never sent to the safety filter or put in notifications, though a name someone types in a message reaches the filter.

No advertising, analytics or tracking. We don't collect contacts, browsing history or a continuous location history, or ask a child for more than the app needs. Nothing a child writes is public: it goes only to the chat and the child's parents. A child's device id and notification token are used only to keep them signed in and notify them.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: stores everything above in the EU (Frankfurt) and runs the server code.
  • Mistral AI (France): the safety filter. It checks messages between children, between a child and a grown-up friend, and grown-ups' posts in children's groups (an invited grown-up, or a parent when another family's child is in the group), with a few recent lines of the same chat. People are labelled "Kid A", "Grown-up B"; phone numbers and emails are masked; other text is sent as written. Drawings in those chats go as the picture alone, with no name or chat. For KidoFool it also writes ideas and questions from the round type, the language, the level and the chat's recent ideas, never anything about the players. Paid service with zero data retention: no training, nothing kept after the check. Not sent: messages and drawings between a child and their own parents, a parent's posts in a group of only their own children, and anything between grown-ups.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (possibly anywhere, including the US): delivers notifications, through Apple to iPhones. They carry no message text, names or places: only phrases like "New message" and the number for the app's icon.
  • Namecheap Private Email (US): sends sign-up and password emails, and holds mail to kidochat@atanyco.com.
  • Apple or Google, if a grown-up signs in with them: they share the grown-up's name and email with KidoChat.

Opening a shared location opens Google Maps.

A few KidoChat staff can see accounts, chats and reports, to look into reports, keep children safe and run the service. They can pause or delete accounts and remove messages that break the terms. A copy of someone's information (below) is exported by staff and sent to that person only.

We don't sell personal information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Profiles and messages: until the account is deleted.
  • Drawings, KidoFool rounds, answers, guesses and votes: like messages. Points: until the player leaves the chat or deletes their account.
  • A scheduled message: until it is sent; one that couldn't be sent, a week. "Typing…" is not stored.
  • Your own message: you can delete or edit it within an hour. In a chat with a child, what was deleted and earlier wordings stay visible to that child's parents in their log, and to staff looking into a report. Between grown-ups only, they are erased (files within about an hour).
  • Videos: deleted 7 days after first watched, or 30 days after sending if never watched; the message stays, marked removed.
  • Locations: a pin stays with its message; SOS and parent-requested locations go after 30 days.
  • Codes: pairing and friend codes last 10 minutes; friend links, co-parent invitations and invite links 7 days, each used once. Expired ones are deleted about a day later. A group's invite link lasts until an admin turns it off or makes a new one.
  • A request to join a group by its link: 30 days after it was made or answered.
  • Reports: kept while we look into them and 30 days after, or until the reporter's account is deleted; the copy of the message stays even if the sender deletes their account.
  • Help requests: with the child's account.
  • An ended group stays, unless KidoChat staff delete it for good: then its messages and files go, also from parents' logs (a report keeps its own copy).
  • Deleting an account deletes its profile, messages and files at once. Exceptions: what a grown-up wrote to someone else's child stays in that child's parents' log, and a grown-up's group posts stay under their nickname, without pictures.
  • An account removed for breaking the terms: only its sign-in email or phone is kept, 5 years, to stop it signing up again.
  • A parent's consent record (who, when, which version): with the parent's account, then 2 years without the name.
  • The record of what staff changed (who, when, what; never message text): 2 years.
  • A notification that reaches a child in quiet hours (never any text): held until they end, 2 days at most.
  • An undecided friend request lapses after 30 days.
  • Per-person message counts (to stop flooding): 2 days.
  • Encrypted backups, which only Atany LLC can open: weekly copies up to a month, copies made before each change up to five weeks.
  • On the phone: a copy of your home screen's lists (names and counts, no message text), removed when you sign out or are removed from the phone.

Your choices and rights

  • Parents delete a child's account on the child's page; any grown-up deletes their own under My profile, or by writing to kidochat@atanyco.com. Deleting a parent also deletes a child who has no other parent.
  • A parent can see in the app everything their child sees and sends: the child's details on the child's page and every message in the log. They can correct the details there, and stop all collection by deleting the child's account.
  • Anyone can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of their information (or their child's) by writing to kidochat@atanyco.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • In the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Database rules decide who can read what: a child sees only their own approved chats, and a file opens only for people allowed to read its message. Connections are encrypted; children's secret codes are stored scrambled. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our server so parents can read their child's chats and the filter can check them. One-time codes are stored as they are, since they expire within minutes or days.
